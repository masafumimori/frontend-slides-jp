# Textbook Case preview

A4 case book for adult training. Warm paper, a serif case in two columns with numbered exhibits, then sans questions whose commentary stays covered until the learner presses 「考えた」. One brick-red accent, rules instead of boxes.

## Palette

- `--accent` #8E2B1E. Kicker, question numbers, chapter tab, the losing number, the pick in the framework, primary buttons.
- `--ink` #1E1C19 text and 1.5px outlines. `--sub` #5E5950 labels, notes, sources.
- `--rule` #CFC8BB and `--hair` #E2DCD0 table and ruled-line strokes. `--accent-tint` #F3E4DF the pitfall note and the framework's target quadrant.
- `--page-bg` #FBF9F4 warm paper on `--desk-bg` #E7E3DA.

## Fonts

`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Serif+JP:wght@200;300;400;500;600;700;800;900&family=IBM+Plex+Sans+JP:wght@100;200;300;400;500;600;700&family=EB+Garamond:wght@400;500;600;700;800&display=swap`

Noto Serif JP 700 for titles and the case prose (22px, two columns). IBM Plex Sans JP for questions, commentary, tables and worksheets. EB Garamond caps for the kicker (`CASE 03`, `FRAMEWORK`) and the question numerals.

## Signature moves

1. Serif case / sans learning: the story reads like a book; everything the learner does is in sans.
2. Think-first box: 「まず自分で考える」 bar with a countdown, 「考えた」 button and a ruled note; the commentary below sits under a dashed cover that keeps its space.
3. Exhibits 表1・表2 with a 2px ink rule under the caption and the computed line under them.
4. One framework per case: a 2×2 with the first counterpart in accent.
5. Ink-headed worksheets (職場ワーク, 学びの記録) that save on the device; a discussion protocol with a 30-minute timer and a teaching note behind its own cover.

## Cover recipe (case opener, 1240×1754)

- Running head at top 60, chapter tab on the outer edge (44×240 at top 220), folio at bottom 52.
- Kicker `CASE 03` at top 132; title Noto Serif JP 700 84px one line; deck serif 27px two lines.
- Meta row between two rules: 対象 / 個人学習 / 集合研修 / 立場.
- 登場人物: two people with 100px initial squares (the protagonist filled accent), then the three counterparts with one-line voices.
- 会社の状況: profile list 470px left, a waterfall of the problem number right (0.90 → −1.44 → −0.12 → −0.66).
- このケースで考える問い: four rows with page and minutes, then a time-plan bar that sums to the stated total.
