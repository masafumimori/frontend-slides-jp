# Whitepaper preview

A4 research whitepaper. White pages between a pale top band and bottom band, navy for structure, one green for the finding. Every number carries a spec strip: question, n, period.

## Palette

- `--navy` #13294B. Cover field, top hairline, headlines, chart base series, current chapter tab.
- `--em` #00806B. The one finding: headline keyword, accent series, category label, numbered-heading numbers, AFTER band.
- `--ink` #1B2430 body text. `--mute` #5B6676 notes, sources, labels.
- `--band` #EDF0F4 top and bottom bands, failure boxes, idle chapter tabs. `--paper` #FFFFFF.

## Fonts

`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=IBM+Plex+Sans+JP:wght@400;500;700&family=BIZ+UDPGothic:wght@400;700&family=Barlow+Semi+Condensed:wght@400;500;600;700&family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500;600&display=swap`

IBM Plex Sans JP 700 for headlines and labels. BIZ UDPGothic for body (proportional UD, reads well on screen). Barlow Semi Condensed 600 for big numbers, kickers and folios. IBM Plex Mono for spec strips, question numbers and n.

## Signature moves

1. Spec strip: a navy-outlined mono strip `Q03 | n=412 | 2026.07 | 架空` under every big number, and at the right of every chart caption.
2. Fixed bands: top 0–120 (8px navy hairline, green category label, document name, chapter number and name), bottom 1634–1754 (logo, underlined CTA link, folio `04 / 06`).
3. Chapter ladder: all chapters stacked on the right edge, 44px wide; the current one navy.
4. Numbered headings `1-1` in green mono over a 2px navy rule.
5. Cases as BEFORE navy band → AFTER green band with three numbers; failures as ✕ boxes on band gray.

## Cover recipe (1240×1754)

- Navy field 0–1130 with faint 10-column rules; logo and `WHITE PAPER · RESEARCH` at top 80.
- Green category label at top 236, title IBM Plex 700 92px two lines at top 308, finding sentence 32px at top 590.
- Business-day ledger at top 806: ten cells BD1–BD10, filled to the measured average (6.3), dashed line at the target (3.0), labels below.
- White lower part: survey outline as a 4-column table (対象, n, 期間, 方法, 内訳) at top 1214; fiction notice and logo at bottom 72.
