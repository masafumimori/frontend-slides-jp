# Textbook Workbook preview

A4 Cornell-note workbook (doc-a4 only). White pages, one deep teal, a pale teal cue column on the left of every note and review page, lined writing space in a pen-like hand font. The learner covers the notes, answers the cue questions, and opens one row to check.

## Palette

- `--accent` #1D5F73. Chapter tab, kicker, Q numbers, cue column rule, S/B/I labels, exercise number blocks, buttons.
- `--accent-tint` #EEF4F6. The cue column background, focus fill. Nothing else.
- `--ink` #1F2427 text. Derived greys: `--ink-2` #4B5459 secondary, `--ink-3` #6D767B notes.
- `--rule` #D3D8DB row rules; writing lines #C9CFD3. `--ng` #A8442F wrong answers only. `--paper` #FFFFFF.

## Fonts

`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=IBM+Plex+Sans+JP:wght@400;500;600;700&family=IBM+Plex+Sans:wght@400;500;600&family=Klee+One:wght@400;600&display=swap`

IBM Plex Sans JP for everything printed. IBM Plex Sans 600 for kickers, Q numbers, folios, S/B/I. Klee One (extra) only for what the learner writes: inputs, writing lines, model answers.

## Signature moves

1. Cornell grid: 340px tinted cue column (Q label + 24px 700 question + 言えた tick), 2px teal rule, notes on the right with fill-in blanks `(n)＿＿`.
2. Cover-the-notes review: every note row covered by a lined sheet; each row opens on its own after answering aloud.
3. Writing lines everywhere at 44px pitch (`.write.l1`–`.l4`), placeholders only on the first instance.
4. Model answers behind a closed box ("書き終えてから開く"); printed as a lined 気づき box unless the learner chooses to print answers.
5. Upside-down answer key in the foot; the one-week review page repeats the cue questions alone.

## First page recipe (unit opener, 1240×1754)

- Running head at top 64, teal chapter tab on the outer edge (top 240, 52×420), folio at bottom 56.
- Kicker `UNIT 3 — INTRODUCTION` at top 140, unit title 60px 700 two lines, lead 26px two lines.
- この単元のゴール: 3px teal rule, three can-do lines (24px) with the page they are taught on at the right.
- 進め方: three outlined cells (page + minutes, 24px name, one-line task).
- 事前の自己診断: three questions, three circle choices each in a row, the lesson page reference at the right, answer key upside down in the foot.
