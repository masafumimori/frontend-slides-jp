# Learning Layered preview

An A4 study book for working adults who learn in short sessions and come back later to look things up. Calm editorial ground, serif headlines, one vermilion accent. The book opens with a map of the whole topic; every topic then reads in three depths, and the margin shows where you are.

## Palette

- `--acc` #B2381F. Depth marks (1–3 squares), the question line, the current node on the map, gates in figures, picked points in charts, expander controls.
- `--ink` #1D1F22 text, band rules, map node numbers, the chapter tab. `--sub` #575B60 captions, sources, running head, restated conclusions.
- `--rule` #D8D3C8 hairlines and idle controls. `--tint` #F2EFE7 hover and the pressed depth. `--paper` #FBFAF6 the warm off-white page. `--mute` #8B8E91 (derived) dashed nodes not in this volume.

## Signature moves

1. Whole-topic map (page 1): a vertical flow of numbered nodes, each with its 30-second conclusion and page range; a cross-cutting topic runs as a tall accent band beside the later steps; a dashed loop shows the feedback path.
2. Three depth bands per topic, each under a 2px ink rule with its depth mark (■, ■■, ■■■) and label: 30秒 結論と図 / 3分 理由と手順 / 深掘り 例外・条件・出典.
3. One depth switch outside the pages (30秒だけ / 3分まで / すべて) that opens or folds every topic at once and is remembered; each folded band keeps its header, a teaser of what it holds and a 開く expander.
4. Mini-map in the left margin (x 96–266) of every topic page: the page-1 map in miniature, the current node filled with the accent; each node jumps to its page.
5. Topics run over two pages: the 30-second page (question, conclusion headline, one large figure) and the continuation, which restates the conclusion before the 3-minute and deep bands.
