# Learning Playbook preview

A4 job aid (実務の手引き) for working adults who must carry out a procedure. Warm off-white paper, one deep blue accent, serif headlines over a UD gothic body. Page 1 is the whole procedure on one sheet; every later page is one step (or two short ones) with a template to copy. Six thumb-index tabs on the right edge, shades of the one accent, find any step on screen or on paper.

## Palette

- `--acc` #1D5873: step numbers, tabs (as six shades `--t1`…`--t6`, 18–100% of the accent), labels, 立て直し tag, done fill, the big number.
- `--ink` #1B1F23 text, the template frame and its black title bar. `--sub` #50575D meta, captions, running head.
- `--rule` #D3CFC6 table rows and week grid. `--tint` #F1EFE8 the label column of the record sheet. `--paper` #FBFAF6.

## Fonts

`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Serif+JP:wght@200;300;400;500;600;700;800;900&family=BIZ+UDPGothic:wght@400;700&family=IBM+Plex+Sans:wght@100;200;300;400;500;600;700&display=swap`

Noto Serif JP 600 for headlines and step titles (editorial, not school). BIZ UDPGothic for body, procedures and templates (UD, reads well while working). IBM Plex Sans for numbers, kickers, folios and tab numbers.

## Signature moves

1. Procedure map on page 1: a table of the steps with number chip, title + one-line description, 4-week bar, 所要, 担当, 成果物 and page.
2. Thumb-index tabs: six 176px tabs at the right edge, same place on every page; the steps on the page stand out solid and wider (66 vs 48px).
3. Step block: number chip 64px + serif title + meta line (時期・所要・担当・成果物) + a 終えた box; then 手順 (numbered, imperative) beside 用意するもの and つまずきやすい点 with a 立て直し tag.
4. Worked templates in a black-titled frame with a コピー button: prompt, record sheet, report.
5. Big-number result line (8.0 時間) under a worked tally, all figures from one script.

## Cover recipe (1240×1754): the procedure map

- Running head top 64 (company + book left, 全体の流れ right), tabs from top 260.
- Kicker 20px Plex caps at 140; headline 62px serif in two lines stating the procedure as a sentence; lead 26px / 1.85.
- 112px space, then the map table (1048 wide, rows about 90px), 112px, この手引きの使い方 in two columns of numbered lines.
- Source line 17px at bottom 112; publisher with 「（架空の教材）」 left at the foot, folio right.
