# Infographic preview

A4 picture-led document in the ISOTYPE tradition. Warm white pages; ink pictograms drawn from one kit on a 48-unit grid; quantities shown as rows of one repeated pictogram with a unit chip (1つ ＝ 1,000世帯). Flat color only.

## Palette

- `--ink` #1F2A30. Pictograms, rules, the ground band, chapter tab, folio tile, body text.
- `--key` #D64933. Fill of the one counted thing the headline names (pictograms, the cover's subject). Never text.
- `--key-text` #B8381F. The same accent for text: headline keyword, key value (5.4:1 on paper).
- `--sea` #2F6A8C. Second category: water, sky-side fields, trains; kicker and chapter numbers (5.5:1).
- `--mute` #5A6268 notes and sources. `--paper` #F8F6F1. Tints are color-mix of sea and ink.

## Fonts

`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+JP:wght@400;500;700;800;900&family=BIZ+UDPGothic:wght@400;700&family=Jost:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap`

Noto Sans JP 800–900 for headlines and labels. BIZ UDPGothic for body (UD forms, as public signage). Jost 600 (geometric, after Futura, ISOTYPE's type) for every number, kicker and folio.

## Signature moves

1. Counted row: one pictogram repeated per unit, remainder cut to its fraction, unfilled slots ghost grey; a bordered unit chip `[icon] 1つ ＝ 200 t` on every row.
2. The kit: 17 `<symbol>`s (person, family, house, factory, truck, shop, leaf, drop, yen, doc, phone, train, tree, sun, bottle, can, box) placed with `<use>`; fill only, no strokes.
3. Scenes: mountains as 45° triangles, a ground line, pictograms standing on it at 72/96/120/144/240px.
4. Frame: running head over a 2px ink rule, ink chapter tab carrying the chapter's pictogram, folio as a 48px ink tile.

## Cover recipe (1240×1754)

- Logo tile (ink square, key leaf) and organisation name at top 84; issue `VOL.04 · 2026.10` right.
- Latin kicker 24px sea at top 236; title Noto Sans JP 900 124px, two lines at top 280, subject word in key-text; 30px sub at top 600.
- Scene svg 0–1240 × 700–1300: three 45° mountains (sea tint, sea 40%), sun 132px top right, trees, house, the factory 240px and two trucks in key, town and a sea-blue train on a viaduct.
- Ink ground band 1296–1754: headline number (38,200世帯) with a paper unit chip, a counted row of 38.2 house pictograms in paper, then publisher and 「架空のデータ」 meta at bottom 56.
